# First session and observation record

**FICTIONAL evaluation dossier. All people, events, logs, quotes, and tools are invented.**

This is a scripted scenario, not a real user study. Independent participants are outside the builder team. A builder is present only where a record explicitly says so.

[O01] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) Independent observer Jo Reed watches Rita assemble a watering rota from the existing volunteer availability sheet in 20 minutes. Rita, who is not on the builder team, says, “Every Sunday I assign the next week's watering shifts.”

[O02] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) At 00:00 Rita selects the normal availability sheet, leaves the clearly optional donation section empty, and chooses Watering rota. By 04:00 she has a correct rota from her own input without any builder help. Jo checks every shift against availability. There is no configuration stall.

[O03] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) Rita stays in her existing spreadsheet and uses her existing availability data. The combined Weekly pack folder and index nevertheless present donation-acknowledgment sections that are irrelevant to her job. She learns which section and files to ignore. Jo observes that these are two workflows collected into one pack, rather than a necessary dependency.

[O04] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) Without an outcome question, Rita says, “Next week's watering shifts are assigned and sent; I did not copy each volunteer into a separate message.” Jo checks the sent rota against the correct export.

[O05] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) Rita deliberately changes one availability date to an impossible date. The tool identifies that cell and prevents that rota export. She corrects the date and regenerates the rota alone, then deletes the trial folder. The source sheet remains intact.

[O06] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) Jo separately observes Owen Park, the garden's bookkeeper and also outside the builder team, use Donation acknowledgments with the existing donation ledger. The correct letters are produced without the watering sheet. Owen says, “I need donor letters; I never prepare the volunteer rota.”

[O07] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-06) In separate interviews, Rita asks for a watering-only workspace and Owen asks for a donation-letter workspace. Rita says, “I never use donation data.” Owen says, “I never use volunteer availability.” Their audiences, source records, and finished outputs differ. Both jobs work when run independently.

[O14] (Fictional record date: 2026-10-12) Rita sends Jo an unsolicited note after week 6: “I reopen it on Sundays without reminders and would miss getting the rota out in one run. I still discard the donation sections.” The communication audit records no post-session builder reminders or posts.
