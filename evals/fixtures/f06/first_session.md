# First session and observation record

**FICTIONAL evaluation dossier. This is a scripted scenario, not a real user study.**

Participants: Nina Vale (the intended user, outside the builder's team) and Jo Reed (independent session observer, also outside that team). The builder is absent for the entire session. Nina has not previously used ShelfNote and uses her own ordinary stock-count CSV.

[O01] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Before opening the tool, Jo records Nina completing last week's restock list in 22 minutes using her stock spreadsheet. Nina: “Every Monday I subtract the counts from our minimums and copy the positive amounts into the order.”

00:00 Nina opens her usual spreadsheet.
00:20 Nina selects Restock and reads the four README steps.
01:00 Nina selects her own stock-count CSV.
02:30 Nina checks the preview's 12 row calculations against her source counts and minimums.
03:40 Nina exports the restock CSV beside the input.

[O02] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Jo verifies that the exported quantities are correct, the original CSV is unchanged, and Nina produced this first result alone during her first session with her own input. There was no builder help, account creation, hidden configuration, or retry.

[O03] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Jo records that Nina used the same spreadsheet, source columns, folder, and Monday collection routine as before. Nina: “I don't have to keep another list or remember a different place to do this.”

[O04] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Nina sends the generated restock order through her usual process. Jo compares it with the source data and confirms it is correct. Nina says without an outcome question: “The order is sent, and I didn't have to copy out the twelve calculations.”

[O05] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Nina opens a copy with `two` in one quantity cell. ShelfNote names the source row, disables export, and leaves the input intact. Nina changes that value to `2`, retries successfully, and deletes the trial output. She does all of this without the builder. Jo verifies the visible error, successful recovery, and undo.

[O06] (Fictional record date: 2026-09-07) At the next weekly stock check, Jo observes Nina generate and send her restock order in 4 minutes from the usual spreadsheet and CSV. All 12 output quantities agree with the source data.

[O07] (Fictional record date: 2026-08-31) Nina independently explains the limit to another workshop volunteer: “It calculates the list from our counts and minimums. If we entered the wrong physical count, we still have to correct that count.” The preview exposes those source values; the tool makes no claim to measure physical stock. This input boundary is documented and visible.

[O14] (Fictional record date: 2026-10-12) After week 6, Nina sends the independent observer an unsolicited note: “I reopened it for Monday ordering, without reminders. I'd miss it if it went away because I'd have to go back to copying every subtraction into the order.” The communication audit in this fictional scenario records no builder messages, posts, invitations, or reminders after the initial week-1 session invitation.
